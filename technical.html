TECHNICAL METHODOLOGY

Introduction

AlgoPilot is an experimental autonomous trading system designed to investigate whether disciplined, software-driven trading can generate sustainable returns in real market conditions.

By replacing emotional decisions with systematic analysis, the project seeks to identify repeatable market opportunities while maintaining controlled risk.

Trading intelligence

AlgoPilot analyses cryptocurrency markets continuously, evaluating price behaviour, momentum, volatility, volume and broader market conditions.

Proprietary algorithms identify potential trading opportunities and evaluate them against predefined entry, exit and risk criteria.

Trading decisions are made systematically, without discretionary human intervention. Trading fees and execution costs are included in performance evaluation.

Specific strategy parameters and signal-generation methods remain confidential.

Risk architecture

Capital preservation is a central design objective. Every proposed trade must satisfy predefined risk constraints before execution.

  • Dynamic position sizing
  • Portfolio exposure limits
  • Controlled leverage
  • Protective stop-loss orders
  • Account-level circuit breakers
  • Independent risk enforcement

The system is prohibited from averaging down or widening protective stops. Unrecognised account exposure prevents new autonomous entries.

Safety and execution

Strategy decisions and risk enforcement operate independently. AlgoPilot cannot control account funding, withdrawals or external transfers. These remain under exclusive human control.

The system establishes and verifies exchange-side trade protection, monitors execution and maintains durable records of trading activity.

Automated safeguards restrict new trading activity when account integrity, operational reliability or predefined risk conditions are compromised.

Human oversight remains an integral part of the architecture.

Experimental method

V1 should remain substantially frozen while an adequate live sample accumulates. Changing parameters after every losing trade would introduce hindsight and overfitting.

Analysis should eventually include:

  • Expectancy
  • Average R
  • Win rate
  • Payoff ratio
  • Drawdown
  • Volatility
  • Trading fees
  • Slippage
  • Market-specific results
  • Long versus short performance
  • Time-of-day effects
  • Performance under different market regimes

Operational failures are also data. Reconciliation failures, exchange behaviour, rejected orders and protection issues matter because a profitable signal is of limited value if the surrounding execution system is unreliable.

Multi-strategy roadmap

The longer-term objective is to develop an ecosystem of independent trading strategies capable of operating across different market conditions.

Candidate strategies undergo historical backtesting, robustness testing and out-of-sample evaluation before being considered for live deployment.

Each strategy will maintain independently versioned rules and performance records.

Ultimately, the project aims to investigate intelligent capital allocation between complementary autonomous trading systems.

Public reporting

The public website is separate from live trading infrastructure. It receives only a sanitised static dataset generated from confirmed production records.

The site has no signing capability, trading credentials, ability to submit orders, or access to current positions or live trading signals.

Public reporting may include completed trades, realised performance, periodic account-balance snapshots and historical performance charts.

Current positions, open orders, stop levels, take-profit levels, signals, wallet information and operational controls remain private.

LESS EMOTION. MORE EQUATION.

A strategy that loses several trades has not necessarily failed. A strategy that wins several trades has not necessarily succeeded.

The value of The Autonomous Account comes from accumulating enough controlled evidence to distinguish genuine behaviour from ordinary market variance.

Experimental autonomous trading system using real capital. Historical results are not indicative of future performance.
